Round 01 · Round-table

Sharing
experiences

One at a time, each of us tells the story of how we work with AI-assisted coding today — honestly, no polish required. The questions ahead are just a current to follow.

How this round flows
A few minutes each
  • We go around the room — everyone speaks.
  • Answer the questions that resonate; skip the rest.
  • No right answers. Beginners and power-users both welcome.
  • Listen for echoes — where do our stories overlap?
01
How long have you been coding with AI?
Days, months, years? How did you start? What hooked you — or didn't?
02
What are you using?

The actual kit — which model, which surface. No wrong answers; we're mapping the room.

Claude OpenAI Codex Copilot / Cursor Something else? Desktop / web CLI / terminal IDE plugin
03
How close are you to "agentic" coding?

From autocomplete, to chatting with a model, to handing an agent a task and letting it run — where do you sit on that spectrum?

Autocomplete Chat / pair Agent on a leash Agent off the leash
04
How often do you reach for it?
Every day, all day? For specific tasks? Only when stuck?
05
What's tripped you up?

The friction, the frustrations, the moments you nearly closed the laptop.

Hallucinated APIs Lost context Reviewing AI code Trust & correctness Tooling & setup
06
Which of those have you solved?
What actually worked? A habit, a tool, a prompt? What's still unsolved?
07
Where is your own practice heading?

The improvements you can already feel coming — in how you work, what you trust the machine with, what you want to get better at next.

08
Solo craft, or shared with your team?

Is this something you each figure out alone — or does your team have shared conventions, tooling, and alignment around how to use it?

Everyone freestyles Loose conventions Shared tooling & rules Still figuring it out
